Corrections vs prompt:
  - 1s is not Binance V; it is sum of all observed aggTrade Qty.
  - Census used REST pages of 1000 (same v as Vision col5), not Vision zip download.
  - Non-native SQLite intervals (e.g. 3d) are skipped: Binance does not own them in the live catalog.
  - 2026-09-06 is not a poison start date.
  - Go encoding/json is case-insensitive: struct json:"v" can bind Binance "V". Production WS parse is a case-sensitive map so Volume is lowercase v only. Archive may still be TOTAL_BASE because Ingress MAX(V, later REST v) lifts to v when v>=V. That is coincidence, not a type converter. MAX remains same-semantic only.
  - Derived TFs are not persisted (catalog Persist=false); aggregation certified by AssembleChildOHLCV fixtures, not archive rows.
  - No historical aggTrade tape: 1s census is producer+persistence stats, not REST 1s parity.
