Archive join: 8783 Stars, 49 relations, the first Star's daily VWEMA has no dot.

Workspace
  selectedStar   StarResearch index. The rail follows that index.
  cursorTime     crosshair time, stored beside it. Moving the crosshair does not clear the Star.
  viewport       noted only. It is not an input to color.

Color
  The server freezes each coordinate's discovery-period range once.
  Signed saturation is the larger of abs(P10) and abs(P90), rank index floor((n-1)*p).
  Levels and widths use the discovery-period rank of that coordinate.
  Holdout Stars are colored with those ranges and do not enter them.
  The browser receives a position in 0..1 and paints a 7px dot.
  Amber is low or negative. Gray is the center. Cyan is high or positive.
  The number stays neutral. Missing has an empty ring and no arrow.
  A real zero keeps 0.00, a gray dot, and a flat arrow when the family is signed.

Path
  Words and ATR numbers only. No cyan/amber.

Chart mark
  On 15m, a neutral vertical line is drawn on the decision candle.
  The card's inspection clock is that candle's close.
  The line is not placed at the close, because that timestamp sits on the next candle.

Corrections
  The relation row does not store its two operands. The panel shows the stored gap and a label. It does not subtract.
  Higher-timeframe RSX minus signal is the stored relation. The state rows do not subtract RSX from signal.
  VWEMA and orange mid use present and finite. ValuesOK is not their gate.
  The live page was not opened. The project rule requires an explicit yes before the browser. Restart the server so /api/research/star-inspection is registered.

Accepted on the chart
  The Star title and Path fold. Side is a green or red dot after the star number.
  State dots stay cyan, gray, and amber. Those three paints are a local preference.
  Arrows sit in one column, dots in the next, numbers flush right to the longest open reading.
  Line squares use the chart's paint color. A relation shows two squares.

Not in this chapter

  research/starstop/star_stops.json stays outside this commit. The selector reads it locally.
