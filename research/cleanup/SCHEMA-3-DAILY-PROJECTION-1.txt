============================================================
5. CORRECTIONS TO THE PROMPT
============================================================

The visibility clock is the 15m bar close, not the
decision time. Decision time in this repository is the
15m open. The last 15m bar of a UTC day opens 15 minutes
before the daily close and closes on that daily close.
Tested: the open does not see that daily bar; the close
does. The archive has 66 Stars on that exact close.
1h and 4h already use this close. Daily uses it too.

The history loader drops only one trailing bar. The
newest daily rows were still open at the frozen as-of.
The certified series stops at open 1790208000000, the
last daily bar whose close is at or before that as-of.
A first run that kept the two later bars produced the
same schema-3 digest. They were not visible. The
certified load does not include them.

Schema 2 does not carry a zero daily block.
Absence exists only on StarSnapshotV3, and only when
the daily series was walked and no bar had closed.
