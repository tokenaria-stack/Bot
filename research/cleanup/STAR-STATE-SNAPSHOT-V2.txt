2. WHAT WAS PROJECTED
============================================================

One ReplayClosedBars walk per timeframe, unchanged.
Three walks per extraction. RSXNode and WozduhNode
remain the only writers.

For 15m, 1h, and 4h, on the causal bar:

  orange mid slope and acceleration
  volume RSI EMA5 value, slope, acceleration
  volume RSI EMA12 value and slope
  VWEMA acceleration
  RSI close value, slope, acceleration
  RSI close channel mid, upper, lower, width, width change
  RSI close EMA7 value, slope, acceleration
  MACD RSI(close)+50 value, slope, acceleration

RSX value, slope, acceleration, signal, and signal slope
on 15m, 1h, and 4h.
The existing 15m RSXMinusSignal field is unchanged.
TV divergence stays the 15m fact.

Slope is value[t] - value[t-1] on that timeframe.
Acceleration is value[t] - 2*value[t-1] + value[t-2].
Units are points per bar of that timeframe.
A missing sample stores 0 with OK false.

Not added: EMA12 acceleration, width acceleration,
signal acceleration, RSI of RSI, RSI HL2, RSI AD,
daily, higher-timeframe TV, alignment, scores,
thresholds.
