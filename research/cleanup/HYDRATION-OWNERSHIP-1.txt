======================================================================
D. WHY INDEX-SHIFT IS IMPOSSIBLE
======================================================================

1. First history uses the same slot list visibility will need. Applying
   visibility does not start a second windowed history for already-present
   columns.

2. updatePlots requires source times. It writes value at store times[i]
   from the response row with that OpenTime. A right-aligned window leaves
   unknown left times as sentinel; it cannot place "bar 1 of the response"
   onto store times[0] unless those OpenTimes are equal.

3. A plots-only payload (no times) does not paste. Existing columns stay.
   Callers that still need a merge (enable fetch) pass fetched.times.
