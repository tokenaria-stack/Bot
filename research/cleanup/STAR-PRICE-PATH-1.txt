STAR_PRICE_PATH_GREEN

PricePath is a read of the 15-minute archive after a Star.
Outcome recipes are not implemented.

Gap stays on the path. It is Fill minus Signal for an up Star, and
Signal minus Fill for a down Star, and only when the next bar is the
true successor. It is the distance between two observed prices.
It is not a barrier and not an outcome.

The smoke extract saw 8787 Stars. The certified window was 8783.
The archive tip has moved. This chapter does not recertify that count.

============================================================
4. RULES
============================================================

The Star bar is not copied.
The first bar is data.NextBarOpen(Star OpenTime, "15m").
A later bar at the wrong open is primary_gap and is not used as the fill.
Archive end on a true boundary is truncated.
Signal is the Star kline close, read from the 15-minute row.
Fill is that successor's open, or absent. Absent does not become Signal.

readBars is an argument. The smoke used 8.

ATR is indicators.ATRSeries(CanonicalATRSpec) on bars through the Star
index. A later bar does not change it. A non-positive result sets
ATROK false and stores 0.

ResolveMinuteTouch is not called by BuildPricePath.
A winning minute stamps that minute's OpenTime.
A minute that touches both levels returns finer_dual and no hit time.
Missing minutes return finer_missing or finer_gap.
The old labeler's 15-minute HitAt is untouched.
