STAR_SNAPSHOT_EXTRACTOR_GREEN

One ExtractStarSnapshots call walks 15m, 1h, and 4h once each.
The deterministic fixture produced 134 Stars.
DAG runners born inside that call: 3.
Star count does not add walks.

Corrections to the implementation prompt:

- Side is the canonical edge string "up" or "down" (nodes.WozduhXoverSideUp / Down). The prompt's UP/DOWN labels are those two values.
- The single slope field is VWEMA(HL2)[t] - VWEMA(HL2)[t-1] on that timeframe. Mid slope is not stored. The sign of VWEMA-to-mid distance is not stored. It is the sign of the stored distance.
- An empty higher-timeframe slice is a caller error. A missing context is a finished walk whose latest close is still after the Star, or no bar has closed yet. The Star row stays. This keeps the three-walk law absolute.
- Two rsx_tv_div facts with different directions at the same ConfirmedAt leave TV unavailable. The extractor does not pick a direction.
- NativePresentAge and Spec2TVPivotAgePrimary are not imported. Age is starIndex - factIndex. The fixture kept 44 ages above 36.

4. FIXTURE
============================================================

Synthetic closed bars from 2024-01-01 UTC.
2560 × 15m, 640 × 1h, 160 × 4h.
defaultRSXSettings (length 14, signal 9, source hlc3, div lookback 90).

  stars          134
  up             67
  down           67
  DAG runners    3 per ExtractStarSnapshots call
  1h finite      133
  4h finite      133
  1h unavailable 1
  4h unavailable 1
  tv present     133
  tv none        1
  tv age > 36    44

The unavailable higher-timeframe row closes before the first 1h close and
before the first 4h close. The test rejects a missing block when an eligible
bar exists. Shifting 1h and 4h into the future keeps all 134 Stars and
clears both higher-timeframe blocks.

Two identical calls return identical rows.
An independent ReplayClosedBars of the 15m slice produces the same
(OpenTime, side) sequence. That fourth walk is the test oracle, not part
of ExtractStarSnapshots.

Age 0 is a fact whose ConfirmedAt is the Star OpenTime, with TVAgeOK true.
No fact is TVDirection "none", TVAgeOK false. The stored age integer is 0
in both cases. The flag is the distinction.
