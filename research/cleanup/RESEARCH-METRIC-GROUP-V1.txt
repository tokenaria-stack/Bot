============================================================
1. VERDICT
============================================================

RESEARCH_METRIC_GROUP_V1_GREEN

Population/Lens catalog and Star inspection use the same
fixed-column tree: father [color] [triangle] [checkbox] [name].
Child rows leave column 1 empty and put color under the triangle.
A line is at most three rows. There is no value child. The line
value stays on the father when the group expands. Expansion only
moves acceleration and slope onto child rows. Timeframe is a
section title above the lines. Microscope right columns stay
accel | slope | dot | value.

f9edf3a attach / Number / Evaluate / ResearchUniverse were not
modified. Comparison / histogram rewrite was not started.
