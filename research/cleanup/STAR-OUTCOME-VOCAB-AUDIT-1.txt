STAR-OUTCOME-VOCAB-AUDIT-1
Date: 2026-09-26
READ-ONLY. No path builder, no recipe, no TP, no SL, no label, no Brain3.

The certified Star snapshot stays frozen. This note says what a later
price-path function may read, and what a recipe may decide.

============================================================
1. VERDICT
============================================================

STAR_OUTCOME_VOCAB_PROPOSED

A Star outcome is a pure reading of price bars after the Star close.
It does not replay Wozduh or RSX. It does not live on StarSnapshot.

Corrections:

- The next 15-minute open is not known at the Star close. It is a fill
  measurement on the path. It is not a snapshot field and not a decision feature.
- Four outcome names are not enough. Timeout, a short archive, a hole in
  the 15-minute series, and an unordered touch are different facts.
  The existing labeler already separates them by reason.
- When 1-minute data orders a 15-minute dual touch, forecast/finer_resolution.go
  still stamps HitAt with the 15-minute OpenTime. A Star path should stamp
  the minute that decided the order.
- forecast/candidate_geometry.go pathExcursion folds a threshold of 1 and 2
  volatility units into the excursion. Those thresholds are a recipe.
  They do not belong on the raw path.
- Do not persist a second copy of future OHLC. The archive is the price
  truth. The path is a function of that archive. A cache may be dropped
  and rebuilt. MFE alone cannot answer a new barrier.
- indicators.ATRValues returns nil until the period is full. The causal
  series is indicators.ATRSeries / UpdateClosed. It is finite from the
  first true range under atr:wilder-rma-first-tr-v1.
- The old structural stop is a recipe: k=2 wick, then 0.15 ATR, then R
  multiples, horizon 72. The wick can be read later. The buffer and the
  multiples stay out of the path.

============================================================
2. DECISION BOUNDARY
============================================================

Owner of the Star clock: the closed 15-minute kline.
Identity: OpenTime in milliseconds.
Decision instant: that bar's CloseTime, which is data.BarCloseTimeMs
of that OpenTime on 15m (next open minus 1).

The edge is knowable only after that close. The high and low of the
Star bar printed before the close. A fill at the close cannot harvest
them. Path extremes start on the following bar.

============================================================
3. TWO ENTRY REFERENCES
============================================================

Signal entry: the Star bar's close. Known at CloseTime.
Fill entry: the open of the next 15-minute bar. Known only once that
bar exists. If the archive has no next bar, the fill entry is absent
and the Star row remains.

Signed gap, signal to fill:

  up:   nextOpen - starClose
  down: starClose - nextOpen

A positive gap is a fill already in the favorable direction.
A negative gap is slippage against the signal.
That gap is not a barrier hit. Barrier hits are high/low after the
chosen entry price.

The old labeler centers barriers on bars[t].Close and never sees a
next-open entry. See forecast/label_set.go labelCandidate. A Star
recipe may use either entry. The path function must take the entry
price as an argument.

============================================================
4. SIGNED PATH
============================================================

One coordinate for both sides. Entry is the price the recipe chose.

  up,   favorable: price - entry
  up,   adverse:   entry - price
  down, favorable: entry - price
  down, adverse:   price - entry

Future bars are the 15-minute bars whose OpenTime is strictly after
the Star OpenTime, in order, and only while each OpenTime equals
data.NextBarOpen of the previous one. A hole stops the walk. It is
not filled.

The first of those bars is also the fill bar when the entry is the
next open. Its high and low are inside the path. Its open is the fill.

Store excursions in price, not in ATR units. ATR is a separate number.

============================================================
5. 15-MINUTE AND 1-MINUTE PRICE
============================================================

15-minute OHLC orders a barrier only when a bar touches one side.
If high reaches the favorable level and low reaches the adverse level,
the 15-minute bar cannot say which happened first.

1-minute futures bars exist in history.db under symbol BTCUSDT:

  count 3689226
  first open 1569031260000
  last open  1790384760000
  open-time steps other than 60 seconds: 0

The certified 15-minute window starts at 1567964700000. The 1-minute
series starts later, about twelve days after that. Early Stars have
no minute bars. Inside the 1-minute span, the stored minutes are
contiguous. This pass did not join each Star to its minutes.

The ordering rule to reuse is forecast/finer_resolution.go resolve:
walk minute opens from the parent open until the next 15-minute open.
Do not call labelCandidate, TargetSpec, or FeatureRow2.

============================================================
6. MFE AND MAE
============================================================

Over a read window of future 15-minute bars, after the chosen entry:

  MFE = maximum favorable extreme
  MAE = maximum adverse extreme
  MFE time = OpenTime of the first bar that prints that MFE
  MAE time = OpenTime of the first bar that prints that MAE

High is the favorable extreme for an up Star. Low is the favorable
extreme for a down Star. The opposite wick is the adverse extreme.

Both extremes may come from the same 15-minute bar. The path records
both and does not order them. Order is a recipe question, asked only
after a favorable level and an adverse level exist.

forecast/structural_stop.go excursionUntilInvalidation stops favorable
excursion at a stop price and then uses 1-minute children. That is a
recipe shaped like the old stop. The raw path does not stop at a level.

============================================================
7. FIRST HIT
============================================================

Owner of the primary walk: forecast/label_set.go firstPassage.

Given upper and lower prices and a horizon in primary bars, it starts
at the bar after t. For each next bar:

  high >= upper and low <= lower -> both, stop
  high >= upper                   -> up first
  low  <= lower                   -> down first
  neither                         -> continue

The last bar of the horizon is included. The next bar is not.
Verified by the horizon test in forecast/label_set_test.go.

On a signed Star path the same walk becomes:

  favorable level first
  adverse level first
  both on this bar
  neither yet

Hit time is the OpenTime of the bar that answered. When minutes
resolve a both-touch, the hit time is that minute's OpenTime.
The current finer resolver returns the outcome and leaves the caller's
HitAt on the 15-minute parent (labelCandidate). A Star path should
not copy that parent stamp.

============================================================
8. SAME-BAR AND MISSING MINUTES
============================================================

Owner: forecast/finer_resolution.go resolve, and the reason constants
in forecast/label_set.go.

  first minute of the 15-minute bar missing -> finer missing
  a later minute inside that bar missing    -> finer gap
  one minute touches both levels             -> finer dual hit
  minutes never touch either level           -> finer inconsistent
    (the 15-minute high/low said both did)

All four stay unordered. None of them picks a side.
A minute that touches only one level resolves the order.

If the recipe does not consult minutes, a 15-minute both-touch stays
unordered. That matches DualHitExcludeAmbiguous. It is a recipe policy.

============================================================
9. ATR AT THE DECISION
============================================================

Owner: indicators.ATRSeries with indicators.CanonicalATRSpec.
Period 14. Method wilder_rma. Logic atr:wilder-rma-first-tr-v1.
First bar's true range is high minus low. Later bars use the previous
close. The value at index t is the update of bar t.

For a Star, ATR is ATRSeries on 15-minute high, low, and close through
the Star bar, inclusive. No bar after the Star close enters it.

The path stores that one number. It does not store a multiple.
A non-positive ATR makes every ATR-scaled recipe unavailable.
The Star and the price path remain.

Do not use indicators.ATRValues for this. That helper returns nil
when the length is at most the period.

============================================================
10. HORIZON
============================================================

Horizon is how many future 15-minute bars a recipe reads.
It is not a field of the Star.

  the walk reaches the horizon with no hit -> timeout
  the archive ends first                    -> truncated
  a 15-minute open is not the next open     -> primary gap

Timeout HitAt in firstPassage is 0. Truncation HitAt is 0.
A hit on the last horizon bar is a real hit.
Those three results must stay distinct. A truncated row is not a
sample of "nothing happened."

No horizon from the old tape is adopted. 72 is StructuralStopHorizon
and Spec2TargetHorizon. It is one possible recipe argument.

============================================================
11. STRUCTURAL LEVEL
============================================================

forecast/price_swing.go LatestCausalPriceSwing reads a k-bar wick
whose confirming bar has OpenTime at or before the entry bar.
The entry price it compares is that bar's close.
That wick is knowable at the Star close when the confirming bar is
the Star bar or an earlier bar.

forecast/structural_stop.go then subtracts or adds
StructuralStopBufferATR15 (0.15) and builds plus-1R, plus-2R, and
plus-3R. Stop owners are rsx_fractal and price_k2. That package also
takes a Feature tape. None of that is the Star path.

A later recipe may pass a price level into the same first-hit walk.
The level has to be computed from bars already closed at the Star.
This audit does not choose the level.

============================================================
12. PATH VERSUS RECIPE
============================================================

Path function, given the archive and one Star:

  decision CloseTime
  signal entry = Star close
  fill entry   = next 15m open, or absent
  signed gap between those two
  ATR at the Star bar
  future 15m highs and lows in the signed frame
  MFE, MAE, and their OpenTimes inside a caller-supplied read window
  the window ends early on truncation or a 15m hole

The read window is an argument so a summary can be computed.
It is not a frozen horizon. The bars themselves stay in the archive.

Recipe function, given that path plus arguments:

  which entry
  favorable distance
  adverse distance
  horizon, not past the read window
  whether to consult 1m on a both-touch
  optional extra level, already causal at the Star

Recipe output:

  favorable first
  adverse first
  timeout
  truncated
  primary gap
  unordered, with the finer reason when minutes were consulted

Changing the distances or the horizon reads the same archive.
It does not replay indicators and does not rebuild StarSnapshot.

============================================================
13. WHAT IS NOT FROZEN
============================================================

Not selected:

  entry choice
  any ATR multiple
  any R multiple
  the 0.15 ATR buffer
  horizon length
  a structural formula
  TP, SL, or a label enum for Brain3

UP_FIRST and DOWN_FIRST stay the old labeler's absolute directions.
A Star recipe speaks in favorable and adverse, using the Star side.
AMBIGUOUS in the old labeler is excluded from fitting. A Star recipe
must say the same for truncated, gapped, and unordered rows:
they are not a third trade outcome.

============================================================
14. REUSE AND DO NOT CALL
============================================================

Reuse as price laws:

  data.BarCloseTimeMs
  data.NextBarOpen
  indicators.ATRSeries with CanonicalATRSpec
  the branch order inside firstPassage
  the minute walk and the four finer reasons inside finerResolve

Do not call:

  labelCandidate or TargetSpec (they bind ATR multiples and the close entry)
  pathExcursion (it binds thresholds 1 and 2)
  excursionUntilInvalidation (it binds a stop)
  StructuralStop, FeatureRow2, FeatureRuntime2
  Brain3

============================================================
15. FINAL CONTRACT
============================================================

Decision time is the Star CloseTime.
OpenTime remains the identity.

The path is a pure read of later OHLC, plus ATR through the Star bar.
Two entries are measured. The next open is not a decision feature.
Excursion is signed in price. MFE and MAE do not order a same-bar pair.

A recipe names entry, two distances, and a horizon, then asks which
came first. A 15-minute both-touch is resolved only by contiguous
1-minute bars, or it stays unordered. Timeout, truncation, and gaps
stay separate.

No multiplier is chosen in this chapter.
