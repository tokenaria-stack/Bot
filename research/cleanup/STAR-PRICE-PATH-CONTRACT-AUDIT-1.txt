STAR-PRICE-PATH-CONTRACT-AUDIT-1
Date: 2026-09-26
READ-ONLY. No path code, no recipe, no TP, no SL, no horizon constant.

Observation chapters stay frozen. This note freezes the price-path
contract those chapters do not contain.

============================================================
1. VERDICT
============================================================

STAR_PRICE_PATH_CONTRACT_GREEN

The path is a bounded read of archived OHLC after one Star, plus the
ATR of the Star bar. A recipe interprets that read. Nothing here
chooses an entry, a distance, or a horizon.

Corrections to the chapter prompt:

- Signing the series by an entry price would freeze that entry.
  One stored path keeps raw future highs and lows, and both entry
  prices. The recipe subtracts. Two entries then share one path.
- MFE time does not depend on the entry. For an up Star the favorable
  extreme is the highest high, whichever constant entry is subtracted.
  The magnitude does depend on the entry. Store the wicks. Derive the
  magnitude when the recipe picks the entry.
- 1-minute bars are not copied into the path. A recipe reads them from
  the archive only for a 15-minute bar that touched both levels.
- The read length is an argument of the path build, not a field with a
  baked number. 72 is not that argument.
- firstPassage stamps a dual touch with the 15-minute OpenTime, and
  labelCandidate keeps that stamp after the minute walk. The new hit
  time is the minute OpenTime when a minute decides the order.

============================================================
2. DECISION VERSUS PATH
============================================================

Known at the Star close, and already on the snapshot or the Star bar:

  OpenTime
  CloseTime
  side, up or down
  the Star bar's close

Not known at the Star close:

  the next bar's open
  any later high or low
  any 1-minute bar inside a future 15-minute bar

The next open is a fill observation on the path. It is not a decision
feature and it is not written back onto StarSnapshot.

============================================================
3. WHERE THE PATH STARTS
============================================================

Identity remains the Star OpenTime.

The first path bar is the 15-minute bar whose OpenTime equals
data.NextBarOpen(Star OpenTime, "15m"). That open is Star CloseTime
plus 1 millisecond when the bar is the true successor.

The Star bar's own high and low stay out of the path. They printed
before the close.

If the archive has no bar at that next open, the fill entry is absent.
The Star close is not copied into it. The path series is empty.
The reason is truncated when the series simply ended, and primary_gap
when a later bar exists at some other open. Those are not the same.

============================================================
4. ENTRIES
============================================================

Signal entry: Star bar close. Always present for a Star that was read
from a closed bar.

Fill entry: open of the first path bar, and only when that bar is the
true successor. Otherwise absent.

Signed gap, only when the fill exists:

  up:   fill - signal
  down: signal - fill

The gap is the distance between the two references. It is not a
barrier hit. Barrier tests use highs and lows after the entry the
recipe selected.

A recipe that asks for the fill when it is absent gets entry_absent.
It does not fall back to the close.

============================================================
5. ONE SIGNED FRAME
============================================================

Side is the only direction input. There is one walker.

  up
    favorable = price - entry
    adverse   = entry - price
  down
    favorable = entry - price
    adverse   = price - entry

Entry is the recipe's choice of signal or fill. The stored bars stay
in raw price so either choice is a subtraction.

============================================================
6. WHAT A BAR CONTRIBUTES
============================================================

Each future 15-minute bar on the path carries:

  OpenTime
  Open
  High
  Low

Close is not required for excursion or for a high/low barrier.
The first bar's Open is the fill.

Excursion uses the wick, not the close.

  up:   favorable wick = High, adverse wick = Low
  down: favorable wick = Low,  adverse wick = High

MFE is the maximum of (favorable wick - entry) over the bars the
recipe's horizon includes, with entry subtracted at read time.
MAE is the maximum adverse wick distance the same way.
The time of MFE is the OpenTime of the first bar whose favorable wick
equals the best favorable wick. Same rule for MAE.
An empty series has no MFE and no MAE. The value is not zero.

A later bar that only ties the extreme does not move the time.

============================================================
7. ATR
============================================================

Owner: indicators.ATRSeries(indicators.CanonicalATRSpec(), highs, lows, closes)
on the 15-minute bars from the archive start through the Star bar,
inclusive.

Spec: period 14, wilder_rma, atr:wilder-rma-first-tr-v1.
The first true range is high minus low. The value at the Star index
includes that bar and no bar after it.

The path stores that price. It does not store a multiple.
If the value is not finite or not positive, ATR is absent.
The OHLC path remains. A recipe that scales by ATR becomes
unavailable. It does not invent a range.

indicators.ATRValues is the wrong helper. It returns nil until the
length passes the period.

The 0.15 ATR structural buffer is not on this path.

============================================================
8. BARRIER ORDER
============================================================

A recipe supplies a favorable level and an adverse level in price
distance from its entry, and a horizon in 15-minute bars no longer
than the path series.

Walk the same branch order as forecast/label_set.go firstPassage,
in the signed frame:

  one wick reaches its level           -> that side first
  both wicks reach their levels        -> unordered on this bar
  neither                              -> continue
  horizon ends                         -> timeout
  series ended first                   -> truncated
  the next open is missing or wrong    -> primary_gap

A single-sided 15-minute touch is stamped with that bar's OpenTime.

An unordered 15-minute bar may be passed to the minute rule.
The minute rule is the loop in forecast/finer_resolution.go resolve:
minute opens from that 15-minute OpenTime until the next 15-minute
open, each open exactly one minute after the last.

  a minute touches one level     -> that side, hit time = that minute OpenTime
  a minute touches both          -> still unordered
  the first minute is missing    -> finer_missing
  a later minute is missing      -> finer_gap
  no minute touches either level -> finer_inconsistent

No branch guesses. The old HitAt, which stays on the 15-minute open
inside labelCandidate, is not the hit time.

Minutes are read from historical_klines symbol BTCUSDT interval 1m.
They are not stored on the path. The 1-minute series in this archive
starts at open 1569031260000, after the 15-minute series, and has no
60-second holes inside its own span. A Star before that start can
still hold a 15-minute path. Its unordered bars stay unordered.

============================================================
9. COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Path states, on the series:

  filled       the requested read completed bar by bar
  truncated    the archive ended on a true successor boundary
  primary_gap  a successor open is missing or not the next open

Fill entry has its own flag. Absent does not change the Star and
does not rewrite the signal entry.

Recipe states, only when a recipe is applied:

  favorable_first
  adverse_first
  timeout
  truncated
  primary_gap
  unordered          15-minute both-touch, minutes not consulted
  finer_missing
  finer_gap
  finer_dual
  finer_inconsistent
  entry_absent
  atr_absent         only if the recipe scaled by ATR

timeout is the only one that means "the horizon elapsed and neither
level printed." The others are not collapsed into it.

============================================================
10. HORIZON AND A READ BUDGET
============================================================

Horizon is a recipe integer. It is not a path constant.

The path builder takes a read length. It copies at most that many
successor bars, and fewer if the archive ends or a hole appears.
A recipe with a longer horizon than the copied series does not invent
bars. The caller builds a longer read from the same archive.
That read is price only.

This contract does not name the integer. Naming 72 here would turn
the old experiment into the path.

============================================================
11. A LATER LEVEL
============================================================

A structural price can be a third distance in the same walk:
favorable, adverse, and that level. The path does not gain a second
engine and does not store the level.

The level has to be a number already fixed at the Star close.
The k=2 wick in forecast/price_swing.go can be such a number.
The stop built from it in forecast/structural_stop.go, with the
0.15 ATR buffer and the R multiples, cannot. That package also
reads a feature tape.

============================================================
12. OWNERSHIP
============================================================

Price truth: historical_klines, symbol BTCUSDT, the futures key.
15-minute bars for the path. 1-minute bars only inside the minute rule.
The certified Star supplies OpenTime, CloseTime, and side.
The Star bar's close is read from the same 15-minute archive row,
not from a snapshot column the current row does not carry.

The path does not call ReplayClosedBars, Wozduh, RSX, Frame,
the chart, FeatureRuntime2, or the labeler.

============================================================
13. MANY RECIPES, ONE PATH
============================================================

One PricePath value can be read by many recipes. Each recipe picks:

  signal entry or fill entry
  a favorable distance
  an adverse distance
  a horizon within the copied series
  whether unordered 15-minute bars consult minutes

Different choices do not rebuild the snapshot and do not replay
indicators. A different entry is a different subtraction on the same
highs and lows. A different horizon is a shorter prefix. Minutes are
read only for bars that need them.

============================================================
14. THE TYPE
============================================================

PricePath

  StarOpen     int64
  StarClose    int64
  Side         string     // "up" or "down"

  Signal       float64    // Star close
  Fill         float64
  FillOK       bool
  Gap          float64    // signed signal to fill; meaningful when FillOK
  GapOK        bool

  ATR          float64
  ATROK        bool

  Bars         []PathBar  // successor 15m bars only
  Complete     string     // filled, truncated, primary_gap

PathBar

  OpenTime int64
  Open     float64
  High     float64
  Low      float64

Not on this type: distances, multiples, horizon, TP, SL, outcome
name, 1-minute rows, structural stop.

OutcomeRecipe is the four choices in section 13 plus the optional
extra level. Outcome is the recipe state in section 9 plus the hit
OpenTime when a side won.

MFE, MAE, and their times are functions of PricePath, the chosen
entry, and the horizon. They are not a second stored series.

============================================================
15. ANSWERS
============================================================

1. The path starts at the next 15-minute open after the Star.
   The Star bar is not in it.
2. Signal entry is the Star close. Fill entry is that next open,
   or absent. Absent does not become the close.
3. Future bars are contiguous 15-minute successors. Minutes are
   consulted only to order a both-touch.
4. Side selects which wick is favorable. One walker.
5. MFE is the best favorable wick distance from the chosen entry.
   MAE is the best adverse wick distance. Empty series: neither.
6. Extreme time is the 15-minute OpenTime of the first bar that
   prints that wick. A one-sided barrier hit uses that 15-minute
   OpenTime. A minute that breaks a tie uses that minute OpenTime.
7. Both wicks on one 15-minute bar are unordered. Minutes walk in
   order. A minute that touches both stays unordered.
8. No minutes: the 15-minute path remains, and the both-touch stays
   unordered. The Star is kept.
9. Path: filled, truncated, primary_gap, and the fill flag.
   Recipe: the states in section 9. Timeout is only a finished
   horizon with no hit.
10. ATRSeries of CanonicalATRSpec through the Star bar. Price units.
    No multiple.
11. PricePath is section 14.
12. The recipe owns entry choice, two distances, horizon, whether
    to read minutes, and an optional extra level.
13. Yes. Same bars, different subtractions and prefixes.
14. Yes. The extra level is another distance in the same walk.
15. The design leaves out label HitAt's parent stamp, pathExcursion's
    1x and 2x cuts, horizon 72, the 0.15 ATR buffer, and UP_FIRST /
    DOWN_FIRST as stored names.
